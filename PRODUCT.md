# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors interested in the work and background of a computer science student. They should be able to explore projects, accomplishments, and education, then contact the portfolio owner about opportunities. No narrower visitor audience has been specified.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that presents the owner's accomplishments, education, and projects. Success means visitors can understand what the owner has achieved, explore their work, and find a clear way to get in touch.

## Brand Personality

Bold, playful, and experimental. The portfolio should express the owner's individuality and curiosity while keeping their work easy to understand.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic portfolio templates and interchangeable presentations that could belong to any student. No specific reference websites have been selected.

## Design Principles

- Let the owner's work lead: make projects, accomplishments, and education the substance of the experience.
- Express individuality: use playfulness and experimentation to create a recognizable personal identity.
- Make exploration rewarding: help visitors discover and understand the work without unnecessary friction.
- Make contact easy: provide a clear path from interest in the work to a conversation about opportunities.
- Keep expression accessible: preserve usability and comprehension as the experience becomes more experimental.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use keyboard navigation, readable contrast, and reduced-motion support as the baseline. Ensure interactive controls have clear focus indicators and meaningful labels. Experimental interactions must remain usable without motion or a pointer.
